Inside the experiment

The jelly is a little spring lattice: each point remembers where its neighbours belong, while gravity and your hand keep asking it to move. Damping decides how much of that motion is absorbed.

Light travels through a softly shaded, layered surface. The knife follows your drawn line; on release, a clean cross-section becomes two independently wobbly pieces. This is a playful material study, not a kitchen simulator.
